Fig. 2A. SLC25A13 protein relative to ACTB in dried blood spots. Each point is one specimen (raw DIA-NN intensity of SLC25A13 divided by that of ACTB in the same specimen, x 10^-4; no imputation, no other scaling);  Bars show the median and interquartile range of specimens in which SLC25A13 was quantified. SLC25A13 was not detected by the DBS DIA workflow in any of the 16 LoF/LoF specimens and was quantified in all 44 other specimens (Fisher's exact test, P = 6.7e-15); these specimens are shown at zero and were excluded from the ratio statistics. Relative to the pooled controls (disease controls and healthy adults, n = 27), the median SLC25A13/ACTB ratio was 0.35 (bootstrap 95% CI 0.28–0.41; Wilcoxon rank-sum P = 5.7e-09) in carriers and 0.28 (0.19–0.39; P = 3.7e-07) in LoF/Mis individuals; the LoF/Mis to carrier ratio was 0.80 (0.54–1.18; P = 0.27). Disease controls and healthy adults did not differ (P = 0.32). Group medians (x 10^-4): disease controls 3.70, healthy adults 3.25, carriers 1.29, LoF/Mis 1.03. Ref/Ref, no SLC25A13 variant detected; LoF, loss-of-function variant; Mis, missense variant. n: disease controls 21, healthy adults 6, carriers 10, LoF/Mis 7, LoF/LoF 16.
